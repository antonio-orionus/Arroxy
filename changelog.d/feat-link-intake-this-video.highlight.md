**This video, or the whole list.** A link to a video inside a playlist, Mix or album now offers This video beside the list on the URL tab, in the Explore toolbar and in its right-click menu, so the video takes one click and the list is one dialog away. The list's dialog still offers Just this video, where it always was, and Interactive Download in the right-click menu asks how many to load first, as on the URL tab. A Mix can now be downloaded as a list too. When the global hotkey takes the video from such a link, its row in Downloads offers the whole list afterwards, without the video you already have.
